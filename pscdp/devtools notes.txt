
    In Chromium's DevTools frontend, devtools_app.html is the primary, full-featured entry point for standard 
    browser debugging, while inspector.html is a legacy/fallback entry point mostly kept for backward compatibility 
    and remote debugging URLs.

    Main DevTools UI: 
    devtools://devtools/bundled/devtools_app.html

    Standalone Inspector Entrypoint: 
    devtools://devtools/bundled/inspector.html

    devtools://devtools/bundled/inspector.html?ws=localhost:9222/devtools/page/<PAGE_ID>

    http://localhost:<DEVTOOLS_FRONTEND_PORT>/devtools/inspector.html?ws=localhost:8080

    Page-level (/devtools/page/<id>)
        Attaches directly to a single tab's renderer. You immediately see the DOM, 
        Console, and Network tabs for that specific page, and the DevTools window 
        closes if that tab closes.
    
    Browser-level (/devtools/browser/<uuid>)
        Attaches to the root browser session. Instead of inspecting a single tab's 
        DOM right away, the UI acts as a browser-wide inspector (similar to chrome://inspect). 
        
        It gives you access to browser-wide domains (like Target, Browser, SystemInfo, 
        and Tracing) and lets you discover, open, or attach to individual page targets 
        from within the same session.
